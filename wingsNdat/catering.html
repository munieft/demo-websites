What we do

Four ways to feed the crowd.

Sylvanie and Lewis bring gourmet barbecue built on traditional Caribbean and African recipes, with homemade flavour in every tray.

01

Live BBQ grilling

We arrive early, set up the grill and cook through the day into the evening. Your guests get chicken, lamb chops and corn hot off the coals, and you get to enjoy your own party.

02

Party trays & drop-off

Trays of jollof, rice n peas, jerk chicken, wingz, curry goat, mac n cheese and salads, delivered hot and ready to serve for parties, offices and family gatherings.

03

Festivals & carnivals

Our red gazebo is a regular at Notting Hill Carnival, Dream Valley and Brunch n Vibes. We bring the full stall, staff and menu to your festival or market.

04

Community events

School summer fetes, firework displays and charity events. We love feeding our community and keep pricing friendly for good causes.

Good to know

Catering FAQs

Is everything halal?+
Yes. All our meat is 100% halal, from the jerk chicken to the lamb chops and suya. We also have vegetarian and vegan options, including chickpea curry and a jackfruit medley.
Which areas do you cater in?+
We're based around Romford and cater across Havering, Barking & Dagenham, Essex and London. We travel further for festivals and larger events. Just ask.
Do you bring your own equipment?+
Yes. For live BBQs we bring the charcoal grill, utensils and everything needed to cook on site. For festivals we bring our branded gazebo and stall setup.
How far ahead should I book?+
As early as you can. Summer weekends and the carnival season fill up quickly. Send us your date and we'll confirm availability straight away.
Can you cater for dietary needs?+
Absolutely. Tell us about allergies, spice levels and dietary needs when you enquire and we'll shape the menu around your guests.
Is there a discount for first bookings?+
Yes. Mention this website when you enquire and get 10% off your first catering booking with us.
