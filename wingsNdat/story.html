Family · Culture · Fire

Three flags. One family grill.

Wingz'N'Dat started as a gazebo and a dream. Today it's a Romford kitchen, a festival favourite, and a love letter to Ghana, St Lucia and Jamaica.

Who we are

Cooked by Sylvanie & Lewis.

We're Afro-Caribbean caterers who grew up on the kind of food that brings everybody to the table: Ghanaian jollof, St Lucian home cooking, and Jamaican jerk smoking away on the drum.

Wingz'N'Dat is our way of sharing that table with London and Essex. We make gourmet barbecue built on traditional Caribbean and African recipes, with homemade sauces, honest portions and a whole lot of love. You'll find us delivering across Romford, grilling at your family's birthday BBQ, or flying our flags at the biggest carnival in Europe.

The journey

From gazebo to carnival.

The early days

Where it started

A green gazebo, a hand-painted banner, and three flags flying high. The first Wingz'N'Dat stall was all heart, built on family recipes and a team in matching red tees.

Back then → Us now

Levelling up

Market by market and festival by festival, the setup grew. The green gazebo became a full trailer, and then the red Wingz'N'Dat gazebo that people now spot from across the field.

28 May 2022

Dream Valley Festival

We set up opposite the VIP entrance and fed a festival crowd from open to close. It was one of our first big stages.

Two years of vibes

Brunch n Vibes

We served the 2nd-anniversary party of Brunch n Vibes at Secret Gardens with jerk, sweet chilli wingz and a vegan jackfruit medley on the menu.

20 August 2024

Live on BBC Radio

We went on air with BBC Radio to talk about our preparations for Notting Hill Carnival. It was a proud moment for a family business.

Carnival 2026

Notting Hill Carnival at 60

Sixty years of culture, music and vibes, and we were there for it. You'd find us on Oxford Gardens at Red Gazebo 217, serving boneless jerk chicken, plantain and slaw.

Carnival weekend

"Yes yes people, follow us to the stand!"

Every August, Notting Hill turns into a sea of flags, sound systems and smoke from the grills. We film the walk so you can find us: under the bridge, past the pastry shop, round the corner, and straight to the red gazebo.
