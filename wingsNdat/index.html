How we cook

Low & slow marinades. High-heat finish.

  • Overnight marinades of scotch bonnet, pimento, thyme, ginger and garlic, blended in-house.
  • Charcoal-grilled for real smoke and char. We never fake it with liquid smoke.
  • 100% halal meat, homemade sauces, and plantain fried to order.
  • Rated 5 (Very Good) for food hygiene, so you can taste the care.
