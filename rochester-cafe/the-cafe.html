The Café

The room

Brick, timber
& Edison glow.

Step in off Rochester Row and the street noise drops away. Exposed London brick, black timber beams crossing a pale ceiling, and dozens of copper-capped Edison bulbs hanging at different heights over the counter.

The counter itself is the centrepiece: fluted walnut lit from beneath in amber, topped with marble and glass cases of pastry. Behind it, shelves of glass jars (chai, matcha, hot chocolate, Earl Grey), silver teapots and a chalkboard in bright pen. Banquette seating runs along the brick wall; outside, grey bistro chairs catch the sun on the pavement.
