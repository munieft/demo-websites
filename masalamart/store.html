Our story

Your one stop Indian grocery store

Walk through our doors at 189–191 High Street and Ganpati Bappa greets you first — garlanded, glowing and watching over aisles filled with the flavours of home. We opened Masala Mart so Barnet, Hadley, Finchley and Potters Bar families could find everything for their kitchens and their celebrations under one roof.

Crates of fresh methi, dudhi and toover dana arrive weekly. Our shelves carry stone-ground atta, whole spices, namkeen, mithai and puja samagri — and our hot counter keeps the veg puffs coming. Everything we sell is vegetarian.

Good to know

Frequently asked

How does Click & Collect work?

Add items to your basket, choose a collection slot and place your order. It's sent to our team on WhatsApp; we pack it fresh and you pay when you collect from 189–191 High Street.

Where do you deliver?

We deliver locally across EN4, EN5, EN6, N2, N3, N11, N12, N14 and N20 — covering Barnet, East Barnet, Finchley, East Finchley and Potters Bar. Delivery is free on orders over £40.

How do I get an MM Card?

Ask at the till on your next visit — it's free. Show it in store or tick “I have an MM Card” at checkout to get member prices.

Is everything vegetarian?

Yes. Masala Mart and Suman's Caterers are pure vegetarian. Ask us about Jain and no-onion-no-garlic options.

Can you order something you don't stock?

Absolutely — WhatsApp us on 07512 345114 and we'll do our best to source it.
