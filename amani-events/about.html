Since 2008

Built by family, grown by word of mouth

Amani Events is a small, family-run styling company set up and run by husband and wife Jey and Vergi. What began as a passion for beautiful celebrations has, over the years, become a name the Tamil community turns to again and again.

Most of our clients still come to us the same way they always have — through a personal recommendation from a family we've styled before. We take that trust seriously. We make it our priority to understand your objectives and work alongside you from concept to completion, so the day feels unmistakably yours.

Our warehouse

We own everything we style with

From our 2,000 sq ft warehouse in Croydon, we keep all of our own décor, props and equipment under one roof.

Owning our equipment means two things for you: we can price competitively, and we can guarantee the best possible outcome on the day — because nothing is left to a third party. Your first consultation is always free, either at our office on London Road or over at the warehouse, where you're welcome to see the props in person.

  • Full inventory of décor, staging and props owned in-house
  • Competitive pricing with no third-party mark-ups
  • Free initial consultation at a time that suits you
