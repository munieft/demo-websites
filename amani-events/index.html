We understand your traditions

From the kolam at the entrance to the marigold thoranam overhead, the details of a Tamil ceremony carry meaning. We honour every one — and style them with a modern, elegant hand.

  • Tamil & Hindu wedding mandapams and stages
  • Manjal neerattu & coming-of-age ceremonies
  • Fresh flower, poo & marigold garland work
  • Traditional lamps, kolam & thoranam styling
