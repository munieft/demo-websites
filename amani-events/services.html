Weddings & Receptions

The full picture — from a grand ceremony mandapam to a styled reception where every flower, drape and light is in its place. We design and build the day you've always pictured, honouring Tamil and Hindu traditions with a modern, elegant hand.

  • Mandapams & stages
  • Reception styling
  • Floral & garland work
  • Backdrops & draping
  • Lighting
02 · சடங்கு

Coming-of-Age & Traditional Ceremonies

Puberty and coming-of-age ceremonies, manjal neerattu and the rituals that mark a family's milestones. We understand what each ceremony needs — the kolam at the threshold, the marigold thoranam overhead, the lamps and the colour — and we style it all with cultural care.

  • Manjal neerattu
  • Puberty ceremonies
  • Kolam & thoranam
  • Traditional lamps
  • Fresh flowers

Birthdays & Parties

First birthdays, milestone parties and garden celebrations — playful, polished and personal. Whether it's an intimate gathering at home or a full party design, we bring the same eye for detail we give our biggest weddings.

  • First birthdays
  • Milestone parties
  • Balloon & floral installs
  • Themed styling
  • Party accessories

  • Venue draping
  • Lighting design
  • Full transformations
  • Indoor & outdoor
