Our Story

Passion Meets Excellence

JGM Events CIC was founded with a simple yet powerful vision: to deliver first-class event experiences and authentic international cuisine that celebrate culture, community and joy.

Led by a dedicated Black woman entrepreneur based in Lambeth, our team specialises in bringing the rich flavours of African and Caribbean cooking together with elegant event design. Whether it's a wedding, birthday, christening, corporate function or community celebration, we treat every event as if it were our own.

As a Community Interest Company, we are committed not only to excellence in service but also to giving back and supporting our local community in Lambeth and beyond.
