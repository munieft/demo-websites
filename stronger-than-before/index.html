Stronger Than Before is a Community Interest Company founded to support women rebuilding their lives after trauma.

We believe a kitchen can be a safe place to land — somewhere to slow down, use your hands, honour where you come from, and make something beautiful again.

“You are not alone. Your story is not over.”

The kitchen
