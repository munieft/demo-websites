Our story

Founded by B.R.A.S.S. Kept alive by volunteers.

Hatfield Community Hall has stood on Hatfield Road since the 1990s, when it was founded by B.R.A.S.S for the benefit of the groups that use it and the local community around it. That arrangement hasn't changed since.

Founded 1990s Volunteer committee Not-for-profit hire

Built for the groups that were already here

Long before "community hub" was a phrase on a council leaflet, B.R.A.S.S set up this hall so that Dagenham's clubs, classes and families would have somewhere reliable to meet. It was never meant to make anyone rich — it was meant to stay open.

More than thirty years on, the same idea still runs the place: a hall held in trust for the people who actually walk through its doors, week after week.

The hall on Hatfield Road, Dagenham

Cleared, cleaned and reset between bookings by the volunteer team

Run by volunteers, for whoever needs the space next

There's no paid management company behind Hatfield Community Hall. A small committee of local volunteers looks after bookings, upkeep and the day-to-day running of the building, in the spirit B.R.A.S.S set out in the 1990s.

Every hire — a christening, a dance class, a committee meeting — goes straight back into keeping the hall standing for the group booking it next.

The principles behind the hall

Open to the whole community

Whatever your background, occasion or budget, the hall is here to be used — that's the founding idea, and it hasn't changed.

Kept plain and affordable

No dressing the hall up as something it isn't. A solid, well-kept space, priced so local groups can actually afford it.

Looked after by the people who use it

The volunteer committee relies on hirers treating the hall with the same care it's kept in for them.
