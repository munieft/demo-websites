Not a venue company. A hall the neighbourhood owns.

Hatfield Community Hall was founded by B.R.A.S.S in the 1990s and has been run by local volunteers ever since — for the benefit of the groups that use it and the wider Dagenham community.

There's no head office, no franchise, no booking agent taking a cut. When you hire the hall, the money goes straight back into keeping the lights on, the floor clean and the doors open for the next group.
