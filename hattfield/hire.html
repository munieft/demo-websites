Hire the hall

One clear, open space — set up however your event needs.

A single main hall with a garden and car park attached, available to hire by the hour, the session, or for regular weekly slots.

What's included

Inside the hire

Everything below is included in a standard hire — nothing extra to hunt down on the day.

Open main hall

A clear-span room, durable easy-clean flooring and full overhead lighting — suited to seated events, dancing or class layouts alike.

Flexible layout

Chairs & folding tables

Stacking chairs and trestle tables are kept on site and included in your hire, ready to lay out for your numbers.

Included

Kitchen & servery access

A working kitchen area for warming, serving and washing up — ideal for parties bringing their own catering.

Included

Garden & grounds

A brick-walled lawn beside the hall, useful for bouncy castles, stalls, smoking areas or simply spilling outside.

Weather permitting

On-site parking

A dedicated car park at the front of the building, with further on-street parking close by.

Included
Good to know

Rates & availability

Hire rates depend on the day, time and length of your booking, and whether it's a one-off event or a regular weekly slot. As a volunteer-run hall, we keep pricing straightforward and fair for the local community.

Tell us your date and roughly how long you'll need the hall, and we'll come back with a clear quote — no obligation.

Get a quote

Before you enquire, have ready:

  • Your preferred date(s), or whether you need a regular weekly slot
  • Start and end time, including setup and clear-up
  • Roughly how many guests you're expecting
