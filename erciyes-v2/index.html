About us

A North London supplier of meat, fruit, vegetables and wholesale food since 2011.

Our story

Erciyes Meat Ltd started back in early 2011, based in North London. Due to unforeseen circumstances the company had to cease trading in 2018, but came back stronger than ever with a new partner towards the end of 2020.

Our Managing Director, Abdullah Furkan Bektas, wasn't going to stop at supplying meat and meat products. He broadened the business into wholesale food, fruit and vegetables, and confectionery.

During the global pandemic we won back the loyalty of our old customers and gained new clients, and the business thrived more than ever. This was mainly down to the loyalty we built with our partners and the pressures on supply and demand.
