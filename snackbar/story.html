Hey! Welcome to Snack Bar

Your local, under the red awning.

Snack Bar is the kind of café every high street needs. Doors open at 7am with the kettle already on, and from then until 5pm it's full English breakfasts, steaming mugs of tea, barista coffees and freshly homemade dinners.

Commuters grab a red cup on the way to Heathway station. Regulars settle in by the window with the paper. Families come for hot lunches, and everyone saves room for a slice of homemade cheesecake. Eat in or takeaway — either way, we serve with a smile.
