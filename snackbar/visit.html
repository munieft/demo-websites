Getting here

Easy to find,hard to leave.

By Tube

Dagenham Heathway (District line) is right on our doorstep — come out onto Heathway and you'll see our red sign on Station Parade.

By bus

Plenty of local buses stop along Heathway by the station. Hop off at Dagenham Heathway and we're a short stroll away.

Takeaway

In a rush? Every meal and drink can be packed to go. Message us on WhatsApp ahead and we'll have it ready.
