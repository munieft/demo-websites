Coffee shop. Licensed bar.

A little independent on Lower Marsh that does both properly.

Number 44 is small at the front. A handful of tables, a proper espresso machine, a back bar that takes up most of a wall and prints on the walls. Walk through and it opens into the garden.

People come for a flat white, stay for a pint, and end up booking the place for their birthday. It is a short walk from Waterloo Station and a long way from the chain pubs around it.

Out the back

The secret garden of Waterloo

Most people walk past without knowing it is here. Sofas and bright cushions by day, festoon bulbs and heaters by night, and a roof that slides shut when London does what London does.

  • Covered

    Retractable canopies overhead, so rain never calls time.

  • Heated

    Outdoor heaters keep it warm when the evenings turn.

  • Lit up

    Festoon bulbs and fairy lights from one wall to the other.

  • Big games

    A projector and screen for the matches worth watching together.
