Good to know

Questions, answered.

How far in advance should I book?

For weddings and large events, the earlier the better: three months or more secures your date. For parties and patty orders, a week is usually plenty. For funerals and repasts we always do our best at short notice. Just call.

What areas do you cover?

We’re based in London and Stevenage and cater across London, Hertfordshire and the Home Counties. For larger events we travel further, so ask us.

How is catering priced?

Per head, based on your menu, guest numbers and service style (drop-off, buffet or fully served). Tell us your budget and we’ll build the best spread we can around it.

Can you cater for vegetarians, vegans and allergies?

Yes. We always include vegetarian and vegan dishes such as rice and peas, run down, plantain and vegetable patties. Tell us about any allergies when you enquire and we’ll talk you through what’s in each dish.

Do you provide staff, equipment and clear-down?

We can. Chafing dishes, hot cabinets, serving staff and full clear-down are all available, or we can simply drop off hot food ready to serve.

Can I order patties in bulk?

Absolutely. Bulk patty boxes are one of our specialities, mixed fillings by the dozen for offices, schools, wakes and parties. Order ahead for collection from Sweet T’s or delivery.
