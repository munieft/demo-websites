Weddings

Your wedding should taste like home. We pair island flavour with the polish of a grand reception: canapés passed on silver, a dressed buffet of chafing dishes labelled by hand, and a team in whites who keep it flowing.

Popular wedding spreads include curry goat, jerk chicken, rice and peas, escovitch fish and our grand canapé platters, finished with traditional rum cake.

“Wi nuh just feed di guests. Wi treat dem like family.”

Christenings & Blessings

After the church, the family comes home hungry. We bring generous, family-style platters that please grandparents and little ones alike: mild curry chicken, macaroni pie, plantain, fresh salads and a celebration cake if you need one.

We can also arrange cake and party platters for dedications, first birthdays and baby showers.

03

Funerals, Repasts & Nine Nights

In our culture, we remember people by gathering, and gathering means food. Whether it’s a repast after the service or a Nine Night with the whole community, we handle the cooking with discretion and care so your family can be together.

We’re flexible with short notice, and we’ll talk you through quantities so no one goes without. Patty boxes, curry and rice, soup and hot drinks: whatever feels right.

“Walk good.” We’ll take care of the kitchen.

Festivals & Street Food

Notting Hill Carnival at 6.30am? We’re there. Street food is where Caribbean cooking was made to shine: jerk smoke, hot patties and a queue that tells its own story.

We bring the mobile set-up, warmers and team for festivals, fairs, sports days and community events.
