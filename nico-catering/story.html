Our story.

Jamaican roots. London heart. A kitchen that cooks for the whole community.

We believe food is how a family says welcome, how a community says congratulations, and how we say goodbye. So we cook every pot like it’s for our own people. Because it is.

Yard food, top-hat service

Proud of where we’re from.

Nico began as Nico Patties: golden Jamaican patties baked by the rack and sold by the box. Word travelled the way it always does in our community, from one party to the next, and the patties became platters, then pots, then full events.

Our look borrows from the photographs of our great-grandparents’ generation: Black gentlemen in top hats and tailcoats, carrying themselves with dignity and style. That’s the spirit we bring to service. The food, though, is pure yard: curry goat, jerk, rice and peas, the dishes that mean Sunday, celebration and home.

Today we cook from our base at Sweet T’s Bakery in Stevenage and cater across London and Hertfordshire, for ten guests or five hundred.

The flavours of home

Four things in every pot.

Pimento

Allspice

The berry Jamaica gave the world. Warm, peppery and the backbone of jerk.

Scotch Bonnet

The fire

Fruity, fierce and used with respect. Whole for fragrance, chopped for heat.

Fresh Thyme

The soul

Bunches of it, stalks and all, in every curry, stew and pot of rice and peas.

Coconut

The comfort

Coconut milk in our rice, our run down and our coco bread. Rich, sweet and soothing.

Community first

More than a kitchen.

We feed people, and we show up for them too.

Hurricane relief

Standing with Jamaica

When the hurricane hit, we backed the donation drive for Jamaica led by Hope Gardens, a memorial garden in West London created by the children and grandchildren of the first-born Jamaican arrivals to W10 and W11. Medical kits, baby supplies, tinned food and more, collected and sent home.

Black History Month

Telling our stories

Every October we share the lives of Black Britons who came before us, like the early 19th-century boxer Jem Wharton. Our heritage is part of our menu, and part of our brand.

Carnival

Before the sound systems start, we’re already there. Carnival is where Caribbean London comes home, and we’re proud to be part of it.
