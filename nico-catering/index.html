Our kitchen, our culture

From the yard to the banquet hall.

Nico started with patties: golden, flaky and baked by the tray. The pots grew bigger, the guest lists grew longer, and now we cater life’s biggest days with the same home-cooked soul.

Every dish carries the flavours we grew up on: pimento, thyme, scotch bonnet and coconut, slow-cooked the way our grandparents did. We serve it with the poise of a top-hat waiter, because your guests deserve both.

Cooked fresh

Marinated overnight and made from scratch for your day.

Served properly

Chafing dishes, linen, staff and a buffet that looks the part.

Ten guests or five hundred. Celebrations, blessings and farewells.
