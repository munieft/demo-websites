How we cook

Two fires, one hatch.

01

The pot

Long-grain basmati layered with spiced chicken, then left to steam until every grain is separate and soaked in flavour. Each portion gets a complete leg with thigh — not a few pieces lost in the rice.

02

The coals

Wings go into a deep red marinade, onto skewers and straight over glowing charcoal. They come off charred at the edges, juicy in the middle and smoky all the way through.
