What the sign says, we bake

Three lines are painted on the board above our window. They are the whole idea of the place.

Speciality sourdough breads

Our sourdough goes into every filled baguette, under every Croque Monsieur and alongside every cheese and charcuterie board.

Viennoiserie pure beurre

All-butter croissants, pain au chocolat, pain aux raisins, apricot oranais and chausson aux pommes. Best with a coffee at the window table.

Pâtisserie fine

Tartes au citron with a mirror glaze, éclairs, canelés, madeleines and macarons, set out fresh in the cabinet each day.

Through the French year

Some things only appear when the calendar says so.

January

Galette des rois

Puff pastry, almond filling and a paper crown for whoever finds the fève.

December, to order

Bûche de Noël

In praline, chocolat or café. Each bûche serves six. Ask at the counter in early December to reserve yours.

When it turns cold

Soupe à l’oignon

French onion soup with cheese croutons and a wedge of our sourdough.
