Our story

Family-run, and quietly obsessed with the details

Chairs on Demand began with a simple belief: that the furniture at an event should look effortless — even though getting it there never is. From our base in Dagenham, we've grown into a hire supplier that couples, planners and venues across London return to, again and again.

We're small enough to answer the phone ourselves and care about every sash, and equipped enough to seat a wedding for hundreds. Whatever the scale, the promise is the same: clean, matched, showroom-condition pieces, on time, every time.
