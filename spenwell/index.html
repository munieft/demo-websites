// Who's doing the work

A family building firm that still answers its own phone.

Spenwell General Builders is run by Nick Spenwell, who has spent over 30 years learning what's possible in building, renovation and refurbishment — usually the hard way, on real jobs, in real Essex houses.

Because the team stays small, the person who quotes your job is often the person who turns up to do it. We take on each phase of a project ourselves — planning, materials, trades, sign-off — rather than passing you between subcontractors.

The goal on every job is the same: good work, finished on time, at a fair price, with no surprises halfway through.

How a job runs, start to finish.

The same four steps whether it's a leaking tap or a two-storey extension — so you always know what's happening next.
