Discover Pakistan

Mughal cities &
mountain kingdoms

From the grandeur of Lahore to the Karakoram highway, Irfan can add tours, hotels and private drivers to any trip home.

Hunza Valley

Turquoise rivers, apricot orchards and Rakaposhi and Passu peaks above the Karakoram Highway.

Lahore

Badshahi Mosque, Lahore Fort, the Walled City and the best food in the country.

Islamabad

Faisal Mosque beneath the Margalla Hills, plus Daman-e-Koh and nearby Murree.

Skardu & Shangrila

Lakeside resorts, Deosai Plains and the gateway to the world's highest peaks.
