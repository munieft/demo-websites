A gentle guide

The four rites of Umrah

Irfan shares a full preparation guide before you fly. Here is a brief overview of the journey ahead.

إحرام

Ihram

Enter the sacred state at the Miqat with intention (niyyah), clean Ihram garments and the Talbiyah.

طواف

Tawaf

Circle the Kaaba seven times anti-clockwise, beginning and ending at the Black Stone, then pray two rak'ahs.

سعي

Sa'i

Walk seven times between the hills of Safa and Marwah, remembering the devotion of Hajar.

حلق / تقصير

Halq or Taqsir

Shave or trim the hair to complete your Umrah and leave the state of Ihram.
