Sample itineraries

Ideas to inspire
your island journey

Every itinerary is tailor-made. Use these as a starting point and Irfan will shape the rest.

Day 1

Arrive Colombo → Negombo

Private transfer from Bandaranaike International Airport, then rest by the lagoon.

Days 2–3

Sigiriya & Dambulla

Climb Lion Rock at sunrise, explore the Golden Cave Temple and join a village safari.

Days 4–5

Kandy

Visit the Temple of the Tooth and Peradeniya Botanical Gardens, and see a cultural dance show.

Days 6–7

Nuwara Eliya → Ella

Tour a tea factory, then take the famous blue train to Ella, the Nine Arches Bridge and Little Adam's Peak.

Days 8–10

South Coast & Galle

Unwind on the southern beaches and stroll the Dutch fort of Galle before your flight home.

Day 1

Arrive → Bentota

Transfer to a beachfront resort on the west coast.

Days 2–3

Bentota river & turtles

Take a Madu River boat safari and visit a turtle hatchery.

Days 4–6

Mirissa & Galle

Go whale-watching (in season), watch sunsets from Coconut Tree Hill and explore Galle Fort.

Day 7

Departure

A relaxed transfer back to Colombo airport along the Southern Expressway.

Days 1–2

Minneriya / Kaudulla

Jeep safari to watch large elephant herds gather by the reservoirs.

Days 3–4

Kandy & the hills

Hill-country culture and tea estates.

Days 5–6

Yala National Park

Morning and evening game drives in search of leopards, sloth bears and crocodiles.

Days 7–8

Udawalawe & coast

One more safari, then beach time before you fly home.
