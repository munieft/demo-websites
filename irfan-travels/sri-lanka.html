Ideas to inspire
your island journey

Every itinerary is fully tailor-made — use these as a starting point and Irfan will shape the rest.

Day 1

Arrive Colombo → Negombo

Private transfer from Bandaranaike International Airport, rest by the lagoon.

Days 2–3

Sigiriya & Dambulla

Climb Lion Rock at sunrise, explore the Golden Cave Temple and a village safari.

Days 4–5

Kandy

Temple of the Tooth, Peradeniya Botanical Gardens and a cultural dance show.

Days 6–7

Nuwara Eliya → Ella

Tea factory visit, then the famous blue train to Ella, Nine Arches Bridge and Little Adam's Peak.

Days 8–10

South Coast & Galle

Unwind on the southern beaches and stroll the Dutch fort of Galle before your flight home.

Day 1

Arrive → Bentota

Transfer to a beachfront resort on the west coast.

Days 2–3

Bentota river & turtles

Madu River boat safari and a visit to a turtle hatchery.

Days 4–6

Mirissa & Galle

Whale-watching (in season), palm-hill sunsets and Galle Fort.

Day 7

Departure

Relaxed transfer back to Colombo airport via the Southern Expressway.

Days 1–2

Minneriya / Kaudulla

Jeep safari to see large elephant herds gather by the reservoirs.

Days 3–4

Kandy & Pinnawala

Hill-country culture and elephant encounters.

Days 5–6

Yala National Park

Morning and evening game drives in search of leopards, sloth bears and crocodiles.

Days 7–8

Udawalawe & coast

One more safari, then beach time before flying home.
