Meet Irfan

A travel agent who picks up the phone

Irfan Travels carries its founder's name, and his personal promise. Mohamed Irfan is based in Hounslow, a short drive from Heathrow, and he plans and books every journey himself.

His specialities are Umrah, helping families and groups make the journey of a lifetime to Makkah and Madinah, and travel to Sri Lanka, Pakistan and India, where he arranges flights, visas, family visits and tailor-made tours. He also books flights, hotels, visas and holidays worldwide.

Flight and hotel prices change every day, so Irfan doesn't publish fixed rates. Instead he quotes each customer personally, looking for the best value at the moment you book. Travellers have rewarded that care with a perfect 5.0 rating on Google.
