Before you book

Will it fit your space?

Most of our castles need a flat area a little larger than the castle itself, plus room at the front for the safety mat and a bit of space around the sides. Not sure? Send us a photo of your garden or hall and we'll help you choose.

  • Allow roughly 3ft extra on every side
  • Access to a standard power socket within ~25 metres
  • Grass, hard-standing or indoor halls all welcome
