Good to know

Frequently asked questions

How far in advance should I book?

As early as you can, especially for weekends and school holidays which get booked up fast. That said, do message us for last-minute dates — we'll always try to help.

Do you deliver and set up?

Yes. Delivery, setup and pack-down are all included across Newham and East London. We'll arrive in good time before your party and run through a quick safety briefing.

Are your castles safe and tested?

Absolutely. Our inflatables are PIPA tested, we're publicly liability insured, and every castle comes with crash mats and secure anchoring. Safety is our number one priority.

What do you need on the day?

A flat area a bit bigger than the castle, plus access to a standard power socket within around 25 metres. No outdoor power? We can bring a generator.

What happens if it rains?

Many of our castles have shower covers, and lots of venues have indoor space. If the weather looks rough, chat to us — we'll help you find the best option for the day.

How do I pay?

We'll confirm the simplest payment method when you book. Just send your enquiry and we'll take care of the details.

Ready to make some noise?

Tell us your date and postcode and we'll get your castle booked in. Weekends fill up fast — grab yours early!
