Our story

Local, reliable and a little bit super

Bouncy Billz started with a simple idea: hiring a bouncy castle should be easy, safe and genuinely fun — for the kids and the grown-ups organising it.

Today we're a trusted name for birthdays, garden parties, school events and community days around Newham. We keep things personal: quick replies, honest advice, and castles that always turn up clean, safe and on time.

Whether it's a back-garden birthday or a big community fair, we bring the same superhero-sized energy every time.
