Meet Irfan

A travel agent who picks up the phone

Irfan Travels carries its founder's name — and his personal promise. Based in Hounslow, a short drive from Heathrow, Mohamed Irfan plans and books every journey himself.

His two great specialities are Umrah — helping families and groups make the journey of a lifetime to Makkah and Madinah — and Sri Lanka, where he arranges flights, visas and tailor-made holidays across the island. Alongside these, Irfan books flights, hotels, visas and holidays worldwide.

Because prices for flights and hotels change every day, Irfan doesn't publish fixed rates. Instead he quotes each customer personally, looking for the best value at the moment you book — and travellers have rewarded that care with a perfect 5.0 rating on Google.
