    Simple guide to Japanese teas

    Our matcha and hojicha come from Uji. If you are not sure which one is for you, tell us what you are after.

    Matcha 抹茶

    Caffeine
    • Bright green powder, strong taste
    • Lots of antioxidants
    • Gives energy, helps you focus

    Try it as or

    Hojicha 焙茶

    Caffeine
    • Roasted tea, brown, nutty taste
    • Low caffeine, will not keep you awake
    • Toasty, easy on the stomach

    Try it as

    Genmaicha 玄米茶

    Caffeine
    • Green tea with roasted rice, light taste
    • Very low caffeine
    • Mild, not bitter, nice after a meal

    Ask at the counter if it is on this week.
