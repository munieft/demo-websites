Our kitchen, your celebration

A family table, laid for hundreds

Every Suman’s spread begins the way it does at home — with fresh ingredients, whole spices and recipes that taste of festivals, family and the comfort of a Gujarati thali.

From the first plate of farsan at a mehndi to the last spoon of shrikhand at a reception, we cook everything purely vegetarian and serve it with the care you would give your own guests. Whether it’s an intimate pooja, a grand Asian wedding or a working lunch for the office, the menu is shaped around you.
