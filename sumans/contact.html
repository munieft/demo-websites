Good to know
Questions, answered

  

   
Is everything you cook vegetarian?+
Yes — Suman’s is a pure vegetarian caterer. Every dish on our menu, including our live wood-fired pizza, is 100% vegetarian.

   
Do you cater outside London?+
We do. We’re London-based and cater for events nationally, so wherever your celebration is in the UK, get in touch with your venue and date.

   
Can you handle dietary requirements and fasting menus?+
Absolutely. We offer a dedicated faradi (fasting) menu and are happy to talk through other dietary needs — just mention them in your enquiry.

   
Can I choose my own dishes?+
Of course. Browse our full menu, tap ＋ on the dishes you love, and they’ll be added to your enquiry automatically. We’ll help you balance the menu for your guests.

   
How much does catering cost?+
Every event is different, so we quote each one individually based on your menu, guest numbers, service style and location. Send us your details and we’ll come back with a tailored quote.

   
Do you sell festive nasto and sweets?+
Yes — around Diwali and other festivals we prepare dry snacks such as chevdo, gathiya, chakri and sev by the kilo, which can be packed and dispatched to you. See the nasto list.
