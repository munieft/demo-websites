How it works

From fire to first bite

We arrive & fire up

Our team sets up the counter at your venue and brings the oven up to a roaring heat.

Dough, stretched to order

Fresh dough is hand-stretched and dressed with sauce, cheese and your guests’ choice of toppings.

Into the flames

Each pizza is fired over wood for a blistered, smoky crust and perfectly melted cheese.

Sliced & served hot

Straight from the oven to the plate — fresh, fast and impossible to resist.

Why guests queue for it

A counter that becomes the centre of the party

  • 100% vegetarian — made in the same pure vegetarian tradition as everything we cook.
  • Real live theatre: guests watch their pizza made, fired and served in minutes.
  • Perfect for weddings, mehndi nights, birthdays and corporate events.
  • Pairs beautifully with our farsan, chaat and Indo-Chinese starters.
