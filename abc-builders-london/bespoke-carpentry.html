The craft

Joinery that fits
your home exactly

ABC Builders provides a broad range of building and carpentry work, and joinery is where it all started. Every piece is measured, cut and fitted for the space it lives in — no off-the-shelf compromises, no awkward gaps.

From alcove wardrobes and understairs storage to full staircases and standalone furniture, we handle the design, build and installation ourselves. Questions about what we can make? Reach out — we're happy to help.

Fitted wardrobes & storage

Storage built
around the room

Floor-to-ceiling wardrobes that use every inch — including the tricky corners and sloped ceilings that shop-bought units can't. Choose the finish, the internal layout and the handles; we make it fit.

Interiors are planned around how you actually use them: hanging, shelving, drawers and shoe storage, all in proportion.

Staircases

Stairs that
anchor a home

New builds, replacements and reconfigurations for loft conversions and extensions. Clean lines, solid construction and safe, comfortable rise — finished to sit right with the rest of your interior.

We work in painted timber, natural wood and mixed materials to match the look you're after.

Fitted units & furniture

Made for the
gap it fills

Media units, home-office joinery, window seats, bookcases and cabinetry — designed for the exact space and painted or finished to your choice of colour.

Because it's built in place, it looks like it was always meant to be there.
