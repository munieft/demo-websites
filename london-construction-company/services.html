Our approach

We build the boring bits properly.

A beautiful finish only lasts if what sits behind it is right. We spend as much care on the tanking, the substructure and the first fix as we do on the marble and the lighting.

Materials are chosen for how they age, not just how they photograph — honed stone, solid timber, microcement, blackened steel and solid brass. The result is a home that still feels considered in ten years.
