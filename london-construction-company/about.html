The studio

Builders who design. Designers who build.

London Construction Company is a small, directly-employed team in Hackney. We started it because too many good homes are let down in the gap between the people who draw them and the people who build them.

Who we are

Rooted in East London.

We are property developers and builders working across Hackney and the surrounding boroughs. Toms-Arbel Ltd trades as London Construction Company, and everything we do carries our name — so we build as if we'll be walking past it for years, because we will.

Bespoke design and build is the whole business, not a side offering. From the first site visit to the final snag, you deal with the people who are actually doing the work.

“The finish everyone photographs is only as good as the work no one sees.”

How we're different

One team, one standard.

Because design and build live together here, decisions get made quickly and honestly. If something on the drawings won't work on site, we tell you at the start — not halfway through, with the walls open.

Our sites are protected, swept and calm. You'll get a single point of contact, regular updates, and a team that treats your home like somewhere people live, not just a job.

What we hold to

The things we won't cut.

Craft first

Substructure, tanking and first fix done right, so the finish has something solid to sit on.

Straight talk

Honest budgets and timelines, and the courage to say when an idea won't work before it costs you.

A calm site

Clean, protected and respectful of your home and your neighbours from the first day to the last.
