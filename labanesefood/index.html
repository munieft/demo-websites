Ahlan wa sahlan · أهلاً وسهلاً

Street food the Beirut way, on the Isle of Dogs

In Lebanon you don't wait for a restaurant to eat well. You find the grill on the corner, the one with the queue and the smoke.

Ours is a gleaming Airstream parked on the green at Skylines Village, strung with warm bulbs and ringed with wooden tables. Chicken thighs go onto the grill marinated, lamb kofte is pressed by hand, falafel is fried to order, and every box leaves the hatch with hummus, crunchy salad and sauce. Everything is halal, and everything is made fresh that day.

How a box is built

Five layers, every time

  1. The base

    Golden turmeric rice or hot chips. Your call at the hatch.

  2. The crunch

    Red cabbage, shredded lettuce, onion, tomato and cucumber, with a pickled chilli on top.

  3. The hummus

    A generous scoop of smooth chickpea and tahini hummus.

  4. The grill

    Chicken, lamb kofte, katsu, falafel or halloumi, cooked to order.

  5. The sauce

    Garlic, chilli or both, poured over while it's hot.
