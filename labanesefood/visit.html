Good to know
Questions

    

      
Is all the meat halal?
Yes. All of our chicken and lamb is halal, and it's marked on the truck's menu boards.

      
What's the difference between medium and large?
Medium is a satisfying meal for one at £8 on the grill menu. Large is £10 and properly generous. A few dishes, like the Mix Kebab, come large only.

      
Do you have vegetarian options?
We have a full vegetarian section: falafel, halloumi, or both, as wraps or boxes from £6. Add extra halloumi to any of them for £1.

      
What comes in a box?
Every box comes with your choice of rice or chips, fresh salad, hummus and sauce. Every wrap comes with salad, hummus and sauce.

      
Can I order ahead?
Yes. Build your tray on the menu page and send it to us on WhatsApp, or call +44 7308 056702. We'll confirm a collection time.

      
Is there somewhere to sit?
There are wooden tables and chairs on the green in front of the truck at Skylines Village, under the string lights.
