THE WOMAN BEHIND THE CROWN

Built by a caterer first, a planner second.

JGM Events was founded by a British-Ghanaian caterer who started by feeding families before she started dressing venues. That order still shows: the food is never an afterthought, and the styling is never a template.

Today JGM Events CIC works right across South London from a Lambeth base — halls, marquees and function rooms filled with gold chargers, rose centrepieces and trays that empty fast. As a Community Interest Company, every booking also feeds back into events and opportunities for the local community.
