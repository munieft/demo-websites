PASSIONATE EVENT PLANNER & INTERNATIONAL CATERER

Jollof that gets debated. Chafing dishes that empty first.

Catering is where JGM Events started, and it's still the heart of the business. Every menu is cooked fresh, kept hot through service, and built around West African staples, Caribbean-leaning sides and continental crowd-pleasers — so there's a full plate for every guest at the table, whoever they are.

Tell us your guest count and any dietary needs, and we'll build a buffet around it — canapés and plated service available on request.
