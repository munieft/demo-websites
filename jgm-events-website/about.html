HOW IT STARTED

A caterer who happened to be brilliant at décor too.

JGM Events was founded in South London by a British-Ghanaian caterer with a habit of cooking for the whole family gathering, whether anyone asked her to or not. Word travelled the way it does in tight-knit communities — one naming ceremony, one milestone birthday, one wedding at a time — until "can you just do the food" turned into "can you do everything."

Today the business trades as JGM Events CIC, a Community Interest Company built on the same instinct that started it: a room only feels right when the food matches it. We still plan that way — decor and catering decided together, by the same team, on the same site visit.
