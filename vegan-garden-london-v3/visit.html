How to spot the table

  1. The market roof

    Come into Greenwich Market from College Approach and walk under the big glass canopy.

  2. The cream sign

    Look up for “Vegan Garden” painted in green script, strung with pastel bunting.

  3. The leaf-print table

    A green leaf-print cloth, the menu on a red ribbon, and a table full of food.
