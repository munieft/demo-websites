About Myrtle & Olive

Inspired by culture. Finished like fine dining.

Myrtle & Olive is a full-service North London catering company specialising in Afro fusion cuisine. Our fine dining approach blends seasonal ingredients, bold flavours and creative presentation with precise, professional service.

Inspired by culture and diversity, we create one-of-a-kind menus that bring your event vision to life.

We’re not your typical caterers. We love what we do, and it shows.
