Looking for a cute venue for your next event in East London?

Laura’s question, and the answer is yes. Ask, and the room can be yours for workshops, supper clubs, launches, birthdays and anything that needs a long table and good light.

It has already held tarot courses, collage nights, drawing socials and wine tastings. There is a kitchen, a bar, movable tables and a wall that photographs well.
