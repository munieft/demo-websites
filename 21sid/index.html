A café with a deck of cards behind the counter.

Concrete ceiling, red pipes, a lime-green espresso machine covered in stickers and a wall of engraved hands doing what Italian hands do. Pink chairs outside. A teal sofa under the bookshelf if you get there first.

Most people come for a flat white and a slab of banana bread. Some stay for a reading, a six-week course, or a night of scissors, glue and the Major Arcana. Laura’s own words for it: a place where you can stay and where you can be.

The person behind it

Laura Lo Faro reads the cards and bakes the cake.

She is a tarologa who works with the Tarot de Marseille, teaches it to beginners, and treats a reading as a conversation about where you are, not a forecast. She is also the one who made your brownie.
