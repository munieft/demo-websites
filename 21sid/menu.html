Shelf and bar

Under the counter is a small Italian pantry: dried pasta, passata, olive oil, tinned tomatoes, jam, Nutella by the jar and bags of coffee to take home.

The fridges hold soft drinks, juices and milk. There is wine and beer, and the board says, in chalk, to ask for a spritz.
