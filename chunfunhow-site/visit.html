Questions we hear at the counter

Can I choose which cup my drink comes in?

Yes. Tell us the design you like when you order and we will use it if it is in stock that day.

Can I change the sweetness and the ice?

Sweetness runs from 100% down to none in five steps, and ice in four. Brown sugar and winter melon drinks are made at a fixed sweetness.

Which drinks have no caffeine?

Osmanthus Fresh Milk, Brown Sugar Fresh Milk and Winter Melon Fresh Milk or Juice. Use the caffeine free filter on the menu to see them.

Do you have oat or soya milk?

Both, for 30p.

Which drinks can be served hot?

Milk teas, the osmanthus and rose series, brown sugar, winter melon, the specials and all fresh teas. They are marked on the menu.

Do you cater for events?

We do: weddings, corporate events and parties. Send the details from the events page.

Do you sell gift cards?

Yes, at the counter.
