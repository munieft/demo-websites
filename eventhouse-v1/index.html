Welcome to Eventhouse

Elegant celebrations, without the venue price tag

We’re a family-run marquee hire team based in Ilford, dressing gardens and outdoor spaces across London and the Home Counties. From an intimate garden dinner to a 200-guest wedding, we bring the whole set-up to you.

Every marquee is clean, waterproof and finished to a standard you’d expect from a hired venue — chandelier lighting, proper flooring, limewash chiavari chairs and dressed tables — while keeping things refreshingly affordable.

  • Free, no-obligation quotes & site advice
  • Delivery, assembly & collection as standard
  • Fully suitable for public events & risk assessed
