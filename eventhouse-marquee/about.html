A little about Eventhouse Marquee

Family-run, locally based, and genuinely proud of every set-up we deliver.

Our story

Big-day beauty, without the big-venue budget

Eventhouse Marquee began with a simple belief: every family deserves a beautiful celebration, whatever their budget.

Working out of the Redbridge Enterprise Centre in Ilford, we’ve grown by word of mouth — one happy garden party, wedding and christening at a time. Our regulars come back because we treat their event like our own: we turn up on time, set up with care, and leave the space looking spectacular.

From a cosy 4×4m gazebo to a 20-metre reception marquee, we handle the heavy lifting so you can focus on the people who matter.
